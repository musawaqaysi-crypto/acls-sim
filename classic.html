Team around the bed

Bed 4
    How to deploy the staff you have
    1Shout for help, activate the code, get the defibrillator. Compressions 30:2 until help arrives.
    2One compresses. The other calls the code, brings the defibrillator, then takes the airway (bag-mask). Swap every 2 minutes.
    3The triangle: Compressor, Airway, Monitor/Defib. The defib operator leads and keeps time.
    4Triangle plus IV/IO/Meds, who also records. One of the triangle still leads.
    5Add a hands-off Team Leader at the foot of the bed, who also keeps the record.
    6Full code team: one person per role. This is the standard to aim for.
    7+Extras stay outside the triangle: relief compressors in a queue, a runner, a CPR coach, family support. Anyone without a role steps back.
